# Privacy Policy for Quebec Compass

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**

**WesLakInc** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Quebec Compass mobile application (the "App").

Quebec Compass is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Service Canada, the Government of Canada, or the Government of Québec. It helps you prepare paperwork that you file yourself on the official websites.

## Information You Enter in the App

The App lets you keep notes, dates and amounts, for example a worksheet for your Employment Insurance report, a next report date, and milestone dates for an immigration file. This information:
- Is stored **only on your device**, and the worksheet and trackers are encrypted
- Is **never uploaded** to us or to any server
- Can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App

We do not receive this information and cannot see, recover or share it.

## Sign-In and Government Websites

The App never asks for, stores, or handles your Social Insurance Number, access codes, passwords or any government credentials. When you choose to open an official website, it opens in your own browser (a Chrome Custom Tab). You sign in and type everything yourself. The App does not fill in, submit, or tap anything on your behalf.

## Optional "Assisted Mode" (Accessibility Service)

Assisted mode is optional and is off unless you turn it on after reading an in-app explanation and giving your consent. When on, the App uses Android's Accessibility Service, limited to web browsers, to read the visible text of the page your browser is showing. It does this only to work out which declaration question you are on and to spot the confirmation date, so it can show the note you wrote for that question.
- The page text is used on your device only and is **not stored, sent or shared**
- The only thing the App keeps is the next report date it finds on a confirmation page. It is saved on your device, not encrypted, is offered to you to confirm, and is never sent to us
- The service never taps, types, fills in, or submits anything
- You can turn it off at any time in the App or in Android's Accessibility settings

## Reminders and Permissions

- **Notifications**: to remind you of reporting and filing dates that you saved
- **Exact alarms**: to sound one alarm on the last day of your filing window
- **Run at startup**: to restore your reminders after your phone restarts
- **Internet**: to show ads and to open links to official websites

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## Advertising

The App displays ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Ads are set to non-personalised, contextual ads only, and the App does not request the advertising ID. AdMob may still collect certain information, such as your approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and diagnostics, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See also [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- Your name, email address, or contact information
- Your Social Insurance Number, credentials or account details
- Your location
- Your notes, dates, amounts or answers
- The text of any page read by Assisted mode
- Payment information (the App is free and has no in-app purchases)

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not have your data to share. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

## Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your Choices

You can turn off notifications and Assisted mode at any time, delete all information stored by the App by clearing its data or uninstalling it, and limit ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads).

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the date above will be updated.
