# Privacy Policy for TetherCast Pro - Screen Mirroring

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

**WesLakInc** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TetherCast Pro mobile application (the "App").

TetherCast Pro mirrors your phone's screen and sound to a TV or receiver (Chromecast, AirPlay or DLNA devices) on your local network, including your phone's own hotspot. Everything the App captures stays between your phone and the device you choose to cast to. We do not run any server that receives your screen, your sound or your network information.

## Information Collection and Use

### Screen Capture
When you start casting, Android asks your permission to capture your screen. The captured screen is encoded on your device and streamed **only** to the TV or receiver you selected on your local network. It is **never recorded, saved, or sent to us or to any server on the internet**.

### Audio Capture (shown by Android as "Microphone")
Android requires the "record audio" permission for an app to capture the sound that other apps play. The App uses it only to send that sound to your TV together with the picture. **The microphone is never used.** The sound is streamed only to the receiver you selected and is never recorded or sent to us. Audio is optional: you can cast video only.

### Location Permission
Android only shows the name (SSID) of your Wi-Fi network to apps that hold the location permission. The App asks for it to:
- Show the name of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to
- Connect to a Chromecast's temporary setup Wi-Fi when you set up a Chromecast

The App **never reads your location** (no GPS or location services are used). The permission is optional and is not needed for casting.

### Wi-Fi, Hotspot and Nearby Devices
To find TVs and receivers, the App reads your phone's local network details (such as its local IP address) and searches your local network for compatible devices. This information is used on your device only and is **never sent to us or to the internet**.

If you use the App to set up a Chromecast, the Wi-Fi network name and password you choose are sent directly to that Chromecast over your local network, with the password encrypted using the Chromecast's own key. They are not sent anywhere else.

### Optional Features
- **Hide notifications while casting**: turns on a "Do Not Disturb" rule owned by the App while you cast and turns it off afterwards. Your own Do Not Disturb settings are not changed.
- **Mute phone while casting**: remembers your volume so it can be restored after casting.
- **Screen off mode**: shows a dark overlay on your phone while casting continues on the TV ("display over other apps" permission).
- **Notifications**: shows a notification while casting, with buttons to stop casting.
- **Start at boot**: after a restart, the App only restores your phone's sound or Do Not Disturb settings if casting was interrupted.

### Local Storage
The App stores the following on your device only:
- Your settings and preferences
- Devices you saved, and the last devices you cast to (name, model and local IP address)
- Hotspot or Wi-Fi network names, and passwords you choose to save, encrypted with the Android Keystore
- A diagnostic log file used for troubleshooting (network addresses and device names, with secrets removed)

This data:
- Remains entirely on your device and is excluded from device backups
- Is never uploaded to any server
- Is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App

### Advertisements
The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings > Privacy options** in the App.

Ads may appear on the screen you are mirroring, and therefore on your TV.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Contact information
- Your screen content or audio
- Your Wi-Fi network names or passwords
- Usage analytics or crash reports

## Third-Party Services

The App uses **Google AdMob** for advertising and **Google's User Messaging Platform** for consent. These services may collect and use certain information, such as device identifiers (for example, the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data, as described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

When you cast to a Chromecast, the Chromecast itself loads Google's receiver software. This is governed by Google's terms for your Chromecast, not by the App.

## Security

Communication with Chromecast and AirPlay devices is encrypted. Some streaming modes used for compatibility with certain TVs (such as DLNA) send the stream unencrypted on your local network, protected by a random access address. Only cast on networks you trust, such as your own hotspot or home Wi-Fi.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.
